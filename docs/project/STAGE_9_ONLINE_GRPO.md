## 1. Scientific Corrections & Architecture Alignment

In accordance with the `AGENTS.md` operating contract and the Master Implementation Pipeline v1.1:

1. **Policy-Environment-Reward Coupling**:
   - The completion generated by the policy directly drives tool calls and multi-agent incident actions.
   - Reward computation is anchored strictly in the centralized reward contract (`config.runtime.evaluate_reward_contract`), verified against independent environment ground truth.
2. **Normalized Group Relative Advantages**:
   - For a group of $G$ rollouts per step, advantage is computed relative to the group mean and standard deviation:
     $$A_i = \\frac{r_i - \\mu}{\\sigma + \\epsilon}, \\quad \\mu = \\frac{1}{G} \\sum_{j=1}^G r_j, \\quad \\sigma = \\sqrt{\\frac{1}{G} \\sum_{j=1}^G (r_j - \\mu)^2}$$
3. **Strict Curriculum Split Isolation**:
   - GRPO training curriculum samples exclusively from the **Train Split** ($|T_{\\text{train}}| = 16$).
   - Scenarios in $T_{\\text{val}}$ (6) and $T_{\\text{test}}$ (6) are strictly quarantined, guaranteeing zero benchmark contamination:
     $$T_{\\text{train}} \\cap T_{\\text{val}} = \\emptyset \\quad \\text{and} \\quad T_{\\text{train}} \\cap T_{\\text{test}} = \\emptyset$$
